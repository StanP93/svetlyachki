Privacy

Updated October 5, 2026

Lumibugs is a free firefly puzzle game made by one developer. There is no sign-up: the game never asks for your name, email or phone number. There are no ads and no real-money purchases yet.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, stars, settings and attempt log are kept in your browser's storage on your device. To erase them, remove the app from your home screen or clear the site data in your browser.

What the game sends to the developer

Anonymous gameplay stats. They show which levels are too hard, where players quit and what is unclear:

  • a random player ID like android-3f7a9c, created by the game and not linked to your name or accounts;
  • device type, OS version, model (on Android), screen size, browser language, game version, and whether the game runs from the home screen;
  • what happens in the game: sessions, the start and result of each attempt with its moves, time, stars, hints and other boosters, in-game fireflies spent, panels and settings opened, and when the game goes to the background;
  • a copy of your saved progress, so it can be restored if it gets lost;
  • technical errors in the game: the error text and the level where it happened, so they can be fixed.

If you opened the game with a personal link that has a name in it (an address with ?t=), that name is sent too.

What is not collected

Your name (except the case above), email, phone number, contacts, photos and location. Your IP address is not stored and is not used to find your city or country.

Where it is stored

In the PostHog analytics service, on servers in the European Union. Only the developer sees these stats and uses them only to make the game better. They are never shared or sold.

How to delete your stats

A contact email will be added here in the next few days. Write to it and include your player ID: you can find it in the game on the map, in Settings, at the bottom. Everything linked to that ID will be deleted.
